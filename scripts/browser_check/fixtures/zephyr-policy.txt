Browser Test Co Remote Equipment Policy

Every full-time employee at Browser Test Co receives a Zephyr-9 ergonomic chair stipend of 640 dollars.
The stipend must be claimed within 45 days of the employee start date by submitting the form EQ-17 to the facilities team.
Unclaimed stipends expire and cannot be carried over to the next calendar year.
Contractors are not eligible for the Zephyr-9 stipend.

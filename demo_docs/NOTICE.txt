# Demo Documents & Corpus Notice

All corporate policy documents, handbooks, letters, agreements, and procedures in this directory (`demo_docs/`) are **synthetic test fixtures**.

### Notice for Due Diligence & Technical Auditors
- **Purpose:** These files are generated by `scripts/generate_demo_docs.py` to test and demonstrate:
  1. Multi-format parsing (`.md`, `.txt`, `.pdf`, `.docx`, etc.).
  2. OCR fallbacks on scanned attachments.
  3. Hybrid vector and BM25 search retrieval over corporate policies.
  4. Scope restrictions (company-wide, department-scoped, and restricted access).
  5. Rule-based template filling with DocGen.
- **Synthetic Entities:** Any references to corporate offices (e.g. London, Austin, Singapore, Dubai, Lahore), regional managers, employees, customer service SLAs, ISO 27001 / SOC 2 certification audits, or legal departments are fictional demonstration data.
- **Zero Production Data:** Deskwise does not store, bundle, or distribute any real customer data, live corporate documents, or private personnel files in this repository.

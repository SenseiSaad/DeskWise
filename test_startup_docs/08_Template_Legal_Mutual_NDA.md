**Owner:** Legal & Founders  
**Instructions:** Replace every [Field name] with the company and counterparty details provided in the request.

## Template

```text
MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Effective Date: [Effective Date]

Between:
1. [Company], represented by [Full Name] ([Your Job Title], [Email]) ("Party A"), and
2. [Partner Company], represented by [Partner Contact Name] ([Partner Contact Email]) ("Party B").

1. PURPOSE
The parties wish to explore a potential business, technical, or commercial partnership regarding [Purpose of Discussion] (the "Purpose") and may exchange confidential technical, financial, customer, or product information ("Confidential Information").

2. OBLIGATIONS OF CONFIDENTIALITY
Each receiving party agrees to:
(a) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
(b) restrict access only to employees or advisors with a strict need to know who are bound by written confidentiality obligations; and
(c) protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care used for its own confidential material, and never less than reasonable care.

3. EXCLUSIONS
Confidential Information does not include information that is publicly available without breach, already lawfully known prior to disclosure, independently developed without reference to Confidential Information, or required to be disclosed by law with prompt prior notice.

4. TERM & RETURN OF DATA
This Agreement remains in effect for [Term Years] year(s) from the Effective Date, and confidentiality obligations survive for 3 years after disclosure. Upon written request, each party shall promptly delete or return all Confidential Information.

5. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of [Governing Law].

SIGNED FOR [Company]:
Name: [Full Name]
Title: [Your Job Title]
Date: [Effective Date]

SIGNED FOR [Partner Company]:
Name: [Partner Contact Name]
Date: [Effective Date]
```

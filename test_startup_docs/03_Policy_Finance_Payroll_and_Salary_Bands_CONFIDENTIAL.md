# Finance, Payroll & 2026 Compensation Bands (STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL)

**Company:** Northstar Cloud Labs  
**Audience:** Finance Department / Admins · **Confidential clearance ONLY**  
**Owner:** Finance & Founders (`finance@northstarlabs.io`)  

---

## 1. Monthly Payroll Schedule
- Salaries are disbursed on the **28th of every month** (or the preceding Friday if the 28th falls on a weekend) via USD wire / local bank transfer.
- Payroll cutoff for overtime, bonuses, and approved expense claims is the **22nd of each month**.

## 2. 2026 Monthly Salary Bands (USD)
| Role / Band | Monthly Base (USD) | Equity Pool Band |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intern / Trainee (Viewer) | $500 – $700 | — |
| Junior Engineer / Operations Associate | $1,200 – $1,700 | 0.05% – 0.10% |
| Mid-Level Full-Stack / Product Engineer | $2,000 – $2,800 | 0.15% – 0.30% |
| Senior Engineer / Department Manager | $3,200 – $4,200 | 0.35% – 0.60% |
| VP / Head of Function | $4,500 – $5,200 | 0.75% – 1.25% |
| CEO / Co-Founders | $5,500 (capped until Series A) | Founder vesting |

## 3. Expense Approval Limits
- **Up to $150:** Department Manager approval.
- **$151 – $1,000:** Finance Manager approval (`finance@northstarlabs.io`).
- **Above $1,000 or recurring SaaS subscriptions:** Requires Founder / Admin sign-off prior to purchase.

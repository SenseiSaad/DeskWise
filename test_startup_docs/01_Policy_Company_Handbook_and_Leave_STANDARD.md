# Northstar Cloud Labs — Employee Handbook & Leave Policy (2026)

**Company:** Northstar Cloud Labs (24-person SaaS startup)  
**Audience:** Everyone (Company-wide · Standard clearance)  
**Owner:** People & Operations (`hr@northstarlabs.io`)  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  

---

## 1. Working Hours & Hybrid Schedule
- **Core collaboration hours:** 11:00 AM – 4:00 PM PKT (Monday to Friday).
- **Hybrid schedule:** Monday–Wednesday in office; **Thursday and Friday are remote-friendly** for all full-time staff and interns.
- Please post a quick morning standup update in your team channel before 11:15 AM.

## 2. Paid Time Off (Leave Policy)
Full-time employees receive the following paid leave each calendar year:
- **Annual / Casual Leave:** **20 paid working days** per year (accrued at 1.67 days/month). Request at least **3 working days in advance** for 1–2 days off, or **2 weeks in advance** for trips of 5+ days.
- **Sick Leave:** **10 paid days** per year. No doctor's note is needed for 1–2 consecutive days; a medical note is required for 3 or more consecutive sick days. Notify your manager before 10:30 AM.
- **Parental Leave:** **12 weeks fully paid** for primary caregivers and **4 weeks fully paid** for secondary caregivers.
- **Bereavement Leave:** Up to **5 paid days** for immediate family.
- **Carry-over:** Up to **5 unused annual leave days** carry over into Q1 of the next year.

## 3. Home-Office & Learning Perks
- **Home-office setup stipend:** One-time **$250 allowance** after completing the 60-day probation period (monitors, chair, keyboard, UPS/internet backup).
- **Learning budget:** Up to **$300 per year** for courses, books, or cloud certifications approved by your manager.

## 4. Daily Meal & Travel Reimbursements
- Late-evening deployment dinner reimbursement: up to **$15** when working past 9:00 PM on a scheduled release.
- Client-visit ride-share / fuel reimbursement: submit receipts within **10 days** using the Expense Application template.
